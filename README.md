# THE SEVENTH LIFE

A 2D pixel-art mystery-adventure made in Unity for the **2026 Unity Game Jam** (theme: *Lucky Number*).
> **Status:** In development (jam entry)
> 
# # About
You wake up in a village where nobody remembers yesterday. Every villager has lived six lives, but their seventh is missing. The number seven protects the village from an ancient spirit, and someone has stolen the seventh bell. Without it, everyone is slowly disappearing.

Find seven lost memories, uncover who took the bell, and choose: restore it, or keep its power for yourself.

**The twist:** every time you fail a puzzle, the village changes. Doors move, clues vanish, and people remember different things. Your failures become part of the story.
